PING
Politics and Internet Working Group

European University Institute · Florence · 2026–27

PING is a new EUI working group on politics and the internet, across disciplines and methods. A place to present ongoing work and get constructive feedback. Meets every other Wednesday, 16:30–17:45, followed by an apéro. All EUI members (faculty, researchers, fellows, visiting fellows) welcome.

Call for presentations

To present, fill out the presenter application form with a tentative title, short abstract, preferred format, and preferred date(s). Deadline: 14 October. The first session (14 Oct) is internal presentations; external presenters from 28 Oct onwards.

Early-career researchers and scholars underrepresented in the field particularly encouraged. Presenters from outside the EUI self-fund travel and accommodation.

Session formats

Each session runs 1h15. Two options:

  • Feedback session — 15 min presentation, 60 min discussion. Early-stage projects, research designs, pre-analysis plans, specific problems.
  • Full presentation — 60 min presentation (interruptions welcome), 15 min Q&A. Advanced drafts, job-market papers.

Scope

Digital technologies and politics, broadly defined:

  • online political communication and discourse
  • social media and political behaviour
  • online subculture and digital socialization
  • platform governance and content moderation
  • misinformation and information ecosystems
  • algorithmic curation and recommender systems
  • digital media effects on attitudes, norms, and participation
  • online mobilization and activism
  • polarization and online interaction
  • political uses of AI
  • political economy of tech platforms and the tech industry

Any method: computational text and network analysis, field and survey experiments, causal inference with observational and digital trace data, qualitative and ethnographic work on online communities, theory.

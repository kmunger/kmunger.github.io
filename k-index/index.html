Methodology, briefly

Corpus: all OpenAlex works, 2000–2025, whose primary topic falls in the subfields Political Science & International Relations, Sociology & Political Science, or Communication. Universe: authors with ≥5 in-scope works and ≥100 in-scope citations. Each feature is computed from the raw corpus, then percentile-ranked. A scholar's k-index is the weighted sum of their percentiles — the weights being the only "methodological choice", which is the point. The "official" weights are chosen by a linear program whose objective is stated out loud, unlike everyone else's.

Caveats: OpenAlex author disambiguation is a model, and it is wrong at exactly the rate you'd expect; books are undercounted; citation counts carry every bias you've read about. The index updates with each OpenAlex vintage and your rank may go down as well as up.

Data: OpenAlex. Concept & weights-as-ideology: Kevin Munger. Nothing on this page is investment advice.

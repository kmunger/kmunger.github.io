How this works

Corpus: OpenAlex works, 2000–2025, with a primary topic in Political Science & International Relations, Sociology & Political Science, or Communication. Universe: authors with ≥5 in-scope works and ≥100 in-scope citations. Each feature below is a percentile rank over the whole universe; the k-index is their weighted sum. The weights are a modeling choice, like every other ranking's — the difference is that these are printed:

Caveats: OpenAlex author disambiguation is imperfect (if your profile is split, so is your score — fix it via ORCID); books are undercounted; citation counts carry the usual biases. The index updates with each OpenAlex vintage; your rank may go down as well as up.
